# Privacy Policy — Sushi Stack

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

> **Developer note (delete before publishing):** this policy reflects the game with rewarded ads (Google AdMob)
> and in-app purchases (RevenueCat) switched on. Review it with your real setup, add your contact details,
> and complete the App Store "App Privacy" and Google Play "Data safety" forms to match.

## What stays on your device
Your progress is saved **only on your device**: coins, levels, stars, purchases, collections, settings and records.
It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Online events marked "(AI)" use computer players and send nothing anywhere.

## Gameplay statistics
The game keeps a small log of gameplay events (for example "run ended at level 12") on your device, to show your
stats, achievements and your own "My funnel" page. **By default it is not sent anywhere.** If you turn on
Settings → Share anonymous stats, these events (with no name, contacts, location or advertising ID) may be sent to
help find where players get stuck. You can turn it off at any time.

> **Developer note:** if you connect an analytics service (for example Firebase Analytics) to the
> "Share anonymous stats" switch, name it here and list it in the Data safety / App Privacy forms.

## Errors and diagnostics
If something goes wrong, the game writes a short error note on your device (Settings → Diagnostics). It stays on
your device unless you choose "Copy report" and send it to us yourself.

## Online features (when switched on)
> **Developer note:** keep this section only once the online server (server/README.md) is running.

The Daily Roll board, friends' scores, the Tower of the World, the country cup and cloud backup use our game
server. For these, the game sends: a random player ID made on your device (not linked to your identity), the chef
name you typed, your scores and layer counts, your chosen country, and, only if you use cloud backup, your save
code. We keep daily entries for 14 days, country-cup results for 4 weeks, and cloud backups for one year after
their last use. **Delete my online data** (Settings → Save) removes your scores and your cloud backup from the
server at any time.

## Save transfer, invites, friends and friend codes
Save codes, invite codes, challenge and ghost codes, and restaurant codes are made on your device and only go where you paste or send them. A restaurant code contains your chef's name and look and your restaurant's layout.
A save code contains your game progress (no personal information). Friends you add with their invite codes are
kept on your device.

Every day the game also keeps a backup copy of your progress on your device (the last two days), so it can be
restored if something goes wrong. It is deleted with the app.

## Highlight clips
If you turn on highlight clips, the game records only its own screen while you play. Clips stay on your device
unless you choose to share one.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers,
approximate location (from your IP address), and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS you can
limit ad tracking in Settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases (Apple / Google, via RevenueCat)
Payments are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.
RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts to confirm what you bought.
See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Notifications
If you allow them, the game schedules local reminders on your device: at most one daily reminder, one when your restaurant's tip jar is full, one before the next Rush Hour, and one when a dish you started cooking is ready. No server is involved.

## Home-screen widget and Live Activity
If you add the widget, the game shares your restaurant name and popularity, tip-jar status, calendar day, level and next chef with it through storage on your
device (an iOS App Group or Android app preferences). The Lock Screen Live Activity shows the same tip-jar status.
None of this leaves your device.

## Camera (Table mode)
If you turn on Table mode, the game shows your camera behind the tower. The camera image is only displayed on your
screen. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Turn Table mode off and the camera stops.

## Sharing
When you share a score, photo, poster, highlight video, Daily Roll grid, song code or boss chef code, it goes only where you choose to send it. A boss chef code contains the chef's look, name and rules you designed.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and ads are requested as general-audience ads.
