# In-app events (App Store) and promotional content (Google Play)

Both stores can feature time-limited events on your store page and in search. Each card below
links to something that really happens in the game, so it's ready to submit once the app is live.
Check the current size and text limits in App Store Connect and the Play Console before uploading.

| Event | Images | When to run it |
|---|---|---|
| Spooky Kitchen | `events/haunted-card.jpg` (1920×1080), `events/haunted-details.jpg` (1080×1920) | Oct 15 – Nov 2 (the Haunted Izakaya theme is free to claim in October) |
| Food Critic Week | `events/critic-card.jpg`, `events/critic-details.jpg` | Any week; the critic visits about one day in three |
| Gauntlet Challenge | `events/gauntlet-card.jpg`, `events/gauntlet-details.jpg` | Any weekend; pair it with a `coinBoost: 2` remote-config value |

## Spooky Kitchen
- **Event name (≤30):** Spooky Kitchen
- **Short description (≤50):** Give your sushi bar a Halloween makeover
- **Long description (≤120):** Claim the Haunted Izakaya theme for free, finish the Spooky Kitchen Pass and earn the Pumpkin plate.
- **Badge:** Special Event · **Purpose:** Appropriate for all users

## Food Critic Week
- **Event name:** Food Critic Week
- **Short description:** Impress the critic for glowing reviews
- **Long description:** The critic books a table at your restaurant. Serve one tough order with 2 stars for a review, coins and new decor.
- **Badge:** Challenge

## Gauntlet Challenge
- **Event name:** Gauntlet Challenge
- **Short description:** Boss after boss. How far can you go?
- **Long description:** No breaks and no perks: beat chef after chef in Gauntlet mode and set a new best this weekend.
- **Badge:** Competition
